# Accounts — Use Cases

Numbered in the order a reader should meet them, not in the order they were written. The order is the life of a minor account: it is made, the child reaches it, it may change hands, and it ends — either by growing up or by being deleted.

- [[01-create-a-minor-account]] — a full account makes an account for a child and becomes its guardian
- [[02-sign-in-to-a-minor-account]] — the child claims the account and signs in, and the guardian can issue the way in again
- [[03-transfer-guardianship]] — a guardian hands a minor account to another full account
- [[04-make-a-minor-account-a-full-account]] — the guardian asks for the child to hold their own account
- [[05-finish-becoming-a-full-account]] — the child finishes it, and nobody else can
- [[06-delete-a-minor-account]] — the guardian ends the account

**The minor-account half of this module is now complete.** The full-account half is not. Three more use cases belong here and are not specified yet: signing up for a full account, signing in to one, and deleting one. Changing what a *full* account holds about the person is open too; the minor case is answered in [[02-sign-in-to-a-minor-account]]. The missing ones come before these in reading order, so expect these numbers to change again — see [[overview]].
