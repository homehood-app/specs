# Household — Use Cases

Numbered in the order a reader should meet them, not in the order they were written.

- [[01-create-a-household]] — a person starts a household and becomes its owner
- [[02-edit-a-household]] — the owner changes the household's data
- [[03-invite-a-person]] — the owner or an organizer invites somebody to join
- [[04-revoke-an-invitation]] — the household withdraws an invitation before it is answered
- [[05-answer-an-invitation]] — the invited person accepts or declines
- [[06-change-a-members-role]] — the owner promotes or demotes a member
- [[07-transfer-ownership]] — the owner hands the household to another member
- [[08-remove-a-member]] — the owner or an organizer takes somebody out
- [[09-leave-a-household]] — a member takes themselves out
- [[10-delete-a-household]] — the owner ends the household
