# Household — Business Rules

## Only an organizer changes the membership

Inviting a person and removing a member are the organizer's authority alone.

- A member who is not an organizer cannot invite
- A member who is not an organizer cannot remove anybody

## Membership starts with an accepted invitation

There is no other way into a household.

- A person is not a member while their invitation is `Pending`
- A person is not a member if their invitation is `Declined`

## A household always has an organizer

- The last organizer of a household cannot be removed

## A household is a closed boundary

- A person who is not a member of a household cannot see or change anything inside it
