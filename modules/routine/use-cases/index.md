# Routine — Use Cases

Numbered in the order a reader should meet them, not in the order they were written.

- [[01-create-a-routine-for-myself]] — a member says a piece of their own work comes back
- [[02-create-a-routine-for-another-member]] — the owner or an organizer gives a member standing work
- [[03-set-a-rotation-on-a-routine]] — share a routine between members, turn by turn
- [[04-do-an-occurrence-of-a-routine]] — the executor does one turn of a routine
- [[05-miss-an-occurrence]] — nobody did it, and the next one is due
- [[06-edit-a-routine]] — change what a routine says, how often it comes round, or who does it
- [[07-pause-and-resume-a-routine]] — stop the occurrences for a while, and start them again
- [[08-end-a-routine]] — stop a routine the household used and does not want any more
- [[09-delete-a-routine]] — remove a routine that never made an occurrence
- [[10-resolve-a-routine-of-a-former-member]] — decide what happens to a routine somebody left behind
